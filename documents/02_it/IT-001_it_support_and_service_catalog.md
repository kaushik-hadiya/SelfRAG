# IT Support and Service Catalog

## 1. How to reach IT
| Channel | Use for | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| **OrbitDesk** (help.orbitra.internal → IT) | All requests and issues | 24x7 submission |
| Slack **#it-help** | Quick questions | 8 AM – 10 PM IST, 8 AM – 6 PM CT (Austin), 8 AM – 6 PM GMT |
| IT hotline | Urgent: can't log in, laptop dead, security issue | **Ext. 4000** / +91-80-4719-4000 (24x7) |
| IT walk-in desk | Hardware swaps, setup | Bengaluru 3rd floor, Pune 6th floor, Hyderabad 9th floor: 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM, Mon–Fri |

Tip: Before raising a ticket, ask **OrbitBot** — most password, VPN and software issues can be self-resolved.

## 2. Ticket priorities and SLAs
| Priority | Definition | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 – Critical | Company-wide or team-wide outage; security incident | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 – High | Single user fully blocked (cannot log in, laptop failure) | 1 hour | 8 business hours |
| P3 – Medium | Degraded but can work (slow laptop, one app failing) | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 – Low | Requests (new software, accessories) | 1 business day | 5 business days |

If your ticket breaches SLA, reply on the ticket with "ESCALATE" – it is routed to the IT team lead automatically.

## 3. Service catalog (OrbitDesk → IT → Request)
| Service | Approval needed | Typical fulfilment |
|---|---|---|
| New software installation (from approved catalog) | None (self-service in **Orbitra App Store**) | Instant |
| Software not in catalog | Manager + Security review | 5–10 business days |
| SaaS application access (Jira, Figma, Salesforce, etc.) | App owner (see IT-004) | 1–2 business days |
| Additional monitor / keyboard / mouse / headset | None (once per 2 years) | 3–5 business days |
| Laptop replacement (damaged) | Manager | 2 business days (from spare pool) |
| Laptop upgrade (performance) | Manager + IT review | 10 business days |
| Mobile phone (eligible roles only: Sales L5+, Support leads, on-call SREs) | L8 | 7 business days |
| Shared mailbox / distribution list | Manager | 2 business days |
| Admin rights on laptop (temporary, 24h) | Security approval | Same day |
| Guest Wi-Fi for visitors | None | Self-service at reception portal |

## 4. IT asset return
When leaving or upgrading, return assets to the IT desk or schedule a courier pickup via OrbitDesk → IT → "Asset Return".
