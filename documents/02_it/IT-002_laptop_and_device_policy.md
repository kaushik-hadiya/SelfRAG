# Laptop, Device and Hardware Policy

## 1. Standard laptops
| Role | Standard device | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering, Data/AI, Design | MacBook Pro 14" (M4 Pro) | 36 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD |
| Product, Security, IT | MacBook Pro 14" (M4) | 24 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD |
| Sales, Marketing, CS, G&A | Dell Latitude 7450 (Windows 11) | 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD |
| Interns | Dell Latitude 5450 | 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD |

Employees can request a Mac instead of a Windows device (or vice versa) with manager approval if the role's tools support it.

## 2. Refresh cycle
Laptops are refreshed every **4 years** (3 years for engineering MacBooks). You will be notified by IT 30 days before eligibility. Early refresh is possible only for performance issues verified by IT.

## 3. Lost or stolen device
1. **Report within 4 hours** — call the IT hotline (Ext. 4000) **and** email security@orbitra.io. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device.
2. File a police report (FIR in India) within 48 hours and upload it to the OrbitDesk ticket.
3. A replacement is issued within 2 business days after the police report is uploaded.
4. **Recovery charges:** first loss with police report — no charge. Second loss within 3 years, or loss due to negligence (e.g., left unattended in a car) — 50% of the book value is recovered.

## 4. Accidental damage
- Report via OrbitDesk → IT → "Hardware Damage".
- Repairs are covered by warranty/insurance. Liquid damage and screen damage: first incident free; subsequent incidents within 2 years — employee pays up to INR 10,000 / USD 150 / GBP 120.

## 5. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
- Personal phones may be used for Slack, email and Okta Verify only after enrolling in **Intune MDM (work profile)**. Orbitra can wipe only the work profile, never personal data.
- Personal laptops must **not** be used to access internal systems, code repositories or customer data.

## 6. Peripherals
Each employee can get: one external monitor (24" standard, 27" for engineering/design), keyboard, mouse, headset, laptop stand — once every 2 years via OrbitDesk.

## 7. Buying your old laptop
When your laptop is refreshed, you may buy the old device at book value (minimum INR 5,000) after IT wipes it. Request within 15 days of the refresh.

## 8. Travel abroad with laptop
Inform IT 5 days before travel to China, Russia or any country on the restricted list; a loaner device will be issued.
