# Company Announcements (Jul – Oct 2026)

**16 Oct 2026 — Diwali celebrations & freeze reminder**
Diwali celebrations in all India offices on **Friday 6 November** (ethnic day, rangoli competition, lunch). Reminder: deployment freeze from 6–10 Nov (ENG-004). Office closed 8–9 Nov as per holiday calendar (Hyderabad closed only on 8 Nov).

**9 Oct 2026 — Q2 FY27 Town Hall**
The Q2 town hall will be on **Thursday 29 October, 5:00 PM IST** (6:30 AM CT / 11:30 AM GMT) in the Bengaluru Auditorium and on Zoom. Submit questions in advance via Slido (link in #announcements).

**1 Oct 2026 — Mid-year review cycle opens**
Self-reviews are open **1–10 October** in PeopleHub. Ratings will be shared by 15 November (HR-PERF-001).

**22 Sep 2026 — Open enrolment for mentorship programme**
Apply on LearnSphere by 15 October.

**5 Sep 2026 — New Bengaluru HQ: Tower 2 opening**
Floors 4–7 of Tower 2 open on **2 November 2026** for Data & AI and Customer Success teams. Desk neighbourhoods will move; check Orbitra Spaces from 26 October.

**18 Aug 2026 — Security: phishing campaign alert**
Several employees received WhatsApp messages impersonating the CEO asking for gift cards. This is a scam — report per SEC-002.

**1 Aug 2026 — Transport update**
Metro shuttle frequency on anchor days increased to every 20 minutes (ADM-002).

**15 Jul 2026 — FY27 Annual Offsite**
The company offsite "Orbit 2027" will be held in **Goa from 21–23 January 2027** for all India employees; US and UK teams have regional offsites in March 2027. Travel will be booked centrally — do not book on your own.

**1 Jul 2026 — USD 100M ARR milestone**
Thank you all! Every employee receives a one-time **INR 25,000 / USD 300 / GBP 250** milestone bonus in the July payroll (employees who joined before 1 April 2026).
