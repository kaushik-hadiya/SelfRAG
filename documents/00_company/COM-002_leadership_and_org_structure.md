## Job Levels (Grades)
Orbitra uses a 10-level framework. Your level is visible in PeopleHub → My Profile → Job Info.

| Level | Typical titles | Band |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate, Trainee, Graduate Engineer | Associate |
| L2 | Engineer I, Analyst, Executive | Associate |
| L3 | Engineer II, Senior Analyst, Senior Executive | Associate |
| L4 | Senior Engineer, Specialist, Assistant Manager | Senior |
| L5 | Staff Engineer, Lead Specialist, Deputy Manager | Senior |
| L6 | Principal Engineer, Team Lead, Manager | Lead |
| L7 | Senior Manager, Engineering Manager, Senior Principal | Manager |
| L8 | Director, Senior Engineering Manager, Distinguished Engineer | Director |
| L9 | Vice President | Executive |
| L10 | CXO | Executive |

Many policies (travel class, hotel limits, referral bonus, approval authority) depend on level. Where a policy says "L1–L5", it includes both endpoints.

## Approval Authority Matrix (summary)
| Request | L6/L7 manager | L8 Director | L9 VP | CFO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Expense claim | up to INR 50,000 | up to INR 2,00,000 | up to INR 10,00,000 | above INR 10,00,000 |
| Purchase request (PR) | up to INR 1,00,000 | up to INR 5,00,000 | up to INR 25,00,000 | above INR 25,00,000 |
| International travel | — | required | — | — |
| New headcount | — | — | required | required |

For USD/GBP the equivalents are: L6/L7 USD 600 / GBP 500; L8 USD 2,500 / GBP 2,000; L9 USD 12,000 / GBP 10,000.

## Escalation path for any unresolved ticket
1. Ticket owner in OrbitDesk (SLA in ADM-003 / IT-001)
2. Team lead of the resolving team (shown on the ticket)
3. Department head (table above)
4. ombudsperson@orbitra.io (only after steps 1–3, or for sensitive matters)
