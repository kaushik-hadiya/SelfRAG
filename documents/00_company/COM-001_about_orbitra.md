# About Orbitra Technologies

## Overview
Orbitra Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Orbitra") is a B2B SaaS company that builds workflow automation and analytics software for mid-market and enterprise customers. The company was founded in **March 2016** in Bengaluru, India by **Ananya Rao** (CEO) and **Vikram Menon** (CTO).

- **Headquarters:** Orbitra House, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru 560103, India
- **Employees:** ~1,450 (as of 30 June 2026)
- **Customers:** 2,300+ paying customers in 38 countries
- **Fiscal year:** 1 April – 31 March (FY27 = 1 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2027)
- **Legal entities:** Orbitra Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (India), Orbitra Inc. (Delaware, USA), Orbitra UK Ltd. (England & Wales)

## Products
| Product | Description | Launched |
|---|---|---|
| Orbitra Flow | No-code workflow automation platform (approvals, forms, integrations) | 2017 |
| Orbitra Insights | Embedded analytics and dashboards on top of Flow data | 2020 |
| Orbitra Connect | Integration hub with 250+ connectors (SAP, Salesforce, Workday, etc.) | 2022 |
| Orbitra Copilot | AI assistant inside Flow that drafts workflows from natural language | 2025 |

See PRD-001 for product details and PRD-002 for release notes.

## Mission
"Remove busywork from every team, so people spend their time on work that matters."

## Values (ORBIT)
- **O – Own the outcome:** Take end-to-end responsibility; don't wait to be told.
- **R – Respect people:** Disagree openly, decide together, never make it personal.
- **B – Bias for simplicity:** The simplest solution that works is usually the right one.
- **I – Integrity always:** Do the right thing even when nobody is watching.
- **T – Think customer:** Every decision starts with the customer's problem.

## Company Timeline
- **2016:** Founded in Bengaluru with 6 people.
- **2017:** Orbitra Flow v1 launched; Seed round of USD 2M.
- **2019:** Series A (USD 15M); Pune office opened.
- **2020:** Orbitra Insights launched; fully remote during the pandemic.
- **2021:** Series B (USD 60M); Austin, Texas office opened (Orbitra Inc.).
- **2022:** Orbitra Connect launched; Hyderabad office opened.
- **2023:** London office opened (Orbitra UK Ltd.); SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.
- **2024:** Crossed 1,000 employees; Series C (USD 120M).
- **2025:** Orbitra Copilot launched; hybrid work policy formalised (see HR-WFH-001).
- **2026:** Crossed USD 100M ARR; new Bengaluru HQ campus.
