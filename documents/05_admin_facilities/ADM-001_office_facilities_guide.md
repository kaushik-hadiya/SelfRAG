# Office and Facilities Guide

## 1. Access and ID cards
- ID cards are issued within 3 working days of joining; temporary badge from reception until then.
- **Lost ID card:** report via OrbitDesk → Admin → "ID card" immediately; the old card is deactivated. First replacement free; subsequent replacements INR 500.
- **Forgot ID card:** collect a day pass from reception with photo ID (max 3 times per month).
- Office access hours: 7 AM – 11 PM. Access outside these hours (and weekends) needs manager approval via OrbitDesk 24 hours in advance.

## 2. Desk and room booking
- Desks are **hot desks** in neighbourhoods by department. Book via **Orbitra Spaces** app (or Google Calendar for rooms).
- Desk bookings open 14 days in advance; unclaimed bookings are released at **11 AM** if you have not badged in.
- Meeting rooms: book in Google Calendar. Rooms not checked into within 10 minutes are auto-released. Max booking 3 hours for large rooms (12+ seats) unless approved by Facilities.
- Large events (town halls, trainings) in the Bengaluru Auditorium (200 seats): request via OrbitDesk → Admin → "Event space" 10 days in advance.

## 3. Cafeteria and pantry
- Breakfast (8:00–10:00) and lunch (12:30–2:30) are **free** on office days in India offices, served by vendor FreshPlate. Pre-register meals in the Orbitra Spaces app by 10 AM to reduce waste.
- Snacks, tea/coffee are free all day.
- Dinner is provided free for employees working after 8 PM (order via app by 6 PM).
- Food complaints: rate on the app or email cafeteria@orbitra.io.
- Austin and London: catered lunch on anchor days (Tue/Wed); kitchen stocked daily.

## 4. Parking
| Office | Car parking | Two-wheeler |
|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru | Limited; allocated by lottery each quarter (apply via OrbitDesk → Admin → "Parking") + priority for pregnant employees and persons with disabilities | Free, first-come |
| Pune | Paid by Orbitra, first-come | Free |
| Hyderabad | Free, first-come (basement 2) | Free |
EV charging: 12 points in Bengaluru, 4 in Pune — book via Orbitra Spaces (max 3 hours).

## 5. Visitors
Register visitors in **Orbitra Spaces → Invite visitor** at least 2 hours before arrival. Visitors must carry photo ID, sign the NDA at reception and be escorted at all times. Guest Wi-Fi is sponsored through the visitor invite.

## 6. Other amenities
- **Mothers' room / wellness room:** each floor (Bengaluru), 6th floor (Pune), 9th floor (Hyderabad).
- **Lockers:** day lockers free; long-term lockers allocated by request.
- **Gym:** Bengaluru HQ has an in-house gym (6 AM – 10 PM); register on Orbitra Spaces. Other locations: use wellness wallet (HR-BEN-001).
- **Lost and found:** reception desk; unclaimed items donated after 30 days.
- **First aid & medical room:** Bengaluru HQ has a doctor on call 10 AM – 6 PM on weekdays. Emergency ambulance: dial ext. 1111 (security control room).

## 7. Facilities issues
AC, cleaning, furniture, printers, washrooms, pest control → OrbitDesk → Admin, or call Facilities helpdesk **ext. 2222**. Response: 2 hours for urgent issues, 1 business day otherwise.

## 8. Emergency
Fire alarm: evacuate via nearest staircase, do not use lifts; assembly point is marked on each floor map. Fire wardens wear orange vests. Security control room (24x7): ext. 1111.
