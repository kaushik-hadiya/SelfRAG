# OrbitDesk – How to Raise a Ticket

OrbitDesk (help.orbitra.internal) is the single portal for requests to IT, HR, Payroll, Finance, Admin/Facilities, Legal and Security. You can also raise tickets from Slack with **/orbitdesk** or by asking **OrbitBot** to "raise a ticket".

## 1. Before raising a ticket
1. Ask **OrbitBot** – it answers policy and how-to questions and can complete some actions (check leave balance, download payslip, reset password link, book a desk).
2. Check this knowledge base.
3. If still unresolved, raise a ticket. OrbitBot pre-fills the category and summary from your conversation.

## 2. Categories and routing
| Category | Typical requests | Resolver group | Response SLA | Resolution SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IT | Access, hardware, software, VPN, MFA | IT Service Desk | see IT-001 | see IT-001 |
| HR | Leave balance correction, letters not generating, policy clarification, profile changes, BGV | HR Ops | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| Payroll | Salary discrepancy, tax declaration, PF transfer, Form 16, salary advance | Payroll | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Benefits | Insurance enrolment, claims escalation, e-card, wellness wallet | Total Rewards | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Finance | Expense query, PR/PO, vendor payment, corporate card | Finance Ops | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Admin | ID card, desk, parking, cafeteria, transport, printer, AC | Workplace | 2 hours (urgent) / 1 business day | 2 business days |
| Legal | NDA, contract review, legal opinion | Legal | 2 business days | NDA: 2 days; contracts: 7–10 days |
| Security | Vendor review, policy exception, suspected incident | Security | 30 min (incidents) / 2 business days | Varies |

## 3. Writing a good ticket
- One issue per ticket.
- Include: what you tried, error message/screenshot, when it started, impact (are you blocked?).
- Mark **"I'm blocked"** only if you cannot work at all; this raises the priority.

## 4. Tracking and escalation
- Track in OrbitDesk → My Tickets, or in Slack (OrbitDesk bot DMs you updates).
- If SLA is breached, comment "ESCALATE" to route to the team lead. See COM-002 for the full escalation path.
- Tickets are auto-closed **5 business days** after resolution if you don't respond; you can reopen within 14 days.

## 5. Feedback
Rate each resolved ticket (1–5). Low ratings are reviewed by the resolver team lead weekly.
