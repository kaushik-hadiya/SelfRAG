# Employee Transport Policy – India

## 1. Shuttle service (free)
- **Metro shuttle (Bengaluru):** between Bellandur office and Kadubeesanahalli / Marathahalli metro stops every 20 minutes, 8 AM – 8 PM on anchor days (Tue–Thu) and every 40 minutes on other weekdays.
- **Pune:** shuttle to Kharadi bypass and Viman Nagar, 8:30 AM – 7:30 PM.
- Hyderabad: shuttle to Raidurg metro station, every 15 minutes.

## 2. Late-night cab (free)
- Employees leaving office **after 9 PM** can book a company cab to home via the **Orbitra Spaces → Cab** module **by 6 PM** the same day (ad-hoc requests after 6 PM via the transport desk, ext. 3333, subject to availability).
- Drop limit: up to 40 km from the office.
- If no company cab is available, take an Uber/Ola and claim actuals under FIN-001.

## 3. Women employees' safety
- Women employees travelling after 8 PM get a cab with a **security guard escort** when they are the first pickup or last drop.
- Cabs have GPS tracking and an **SOS button** in the app that alerts the security control room (ext. 1111).
- The driver must wait until the employee confirms reaching home via the app ("I'm home").

## 4. Early shift / night shift (Support)
Customer Support night shift employees get two-way cab pickup and drop.

## 5. Complaints
Transport complaints (rash driving, delay, behaviour): Orbitra Spaces app → Cab → "Report issue" or transport@orbitra.io. Safety complaints are escalated to the Head of Workplace within 1 hour.
