# Pricing, Discounts and Deal Desk

## 1. List prices
See PRD-001 §5. INR pricing for India customers: Starter INR 799, Growth INR 1,899 per user/month (GST extra). Annual prepay is the default; monthly billing has a 15% premium.

## 2. Discount approval matrix
| Total discount off list | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account Executive (self-approve in CPQ) |
| 10.1% – 20% | Regional Sales Director |
| 20.1% – 30% | VP Sales (region) |
| 30.1% – 40% | CRO + Deal Desk |
| Above 40% | CRO + CFO |

Multi-year deals (3 years, prepaid) can get an additional 5% without extra approval.

## 3. Non-standard terms (need Deal Desk)
Payment terms longer than Net 30, termination for convenience, uncapped liability, custom SLAs, MFN clauses, source-code escrow. Submit in Salesforce → Opportunity → "Deal Desk Request". Deal Desk SLA: 1 business day. Legal review per LEG-001.

## 4. Trials and POCs
- Self-serve free trial: 14 days on Growth features.
- Enterprise POC: up to 30 days, requires a signed POC agreement and success criteria; extensions need the RSD's approval.

## 5. Special pricing programmes
- **Non-profits and educational institutions:** 30% off list (approved by Deal Desk with proof of status).
- **Startups (< 50 employees, < USD 5M raised):** Growth plan at Starter price for year 1.

## 6. Sales compensation (summary)
Commission plans are issued annually in April (personal plan letters in PeopleHub). Commission is paid **quarterly**, in the month after quarter close, on **cash collected**. Disputes → salescomp@orbitra.io within 30 days of the statement.
