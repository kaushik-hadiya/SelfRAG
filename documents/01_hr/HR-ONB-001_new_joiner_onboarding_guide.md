# Welcome to Orbitra! – Onboarding Guide

## Before day 1
- You receive a personal-email invite to **PeopleHub** 7 days before joining. Complete: personal details, bank account, PAN/Aadhaar (India) or I-9 (US) or Right-to-Work (UK), emergency contact, and nominee details.
- Background verification (BGV) by **VeriTrust** must be completed; joining can be deferred if BGV documents are pending.
- Your laptop is shipped to your home (remote joiners) or handed over on Day 1 (office joiners).

## Day 1 (Mondays only — Orbitra joining days are Mondays)
| Time (IST) | Activity |
|---|---|
| 9:30 AM | Reception check-in, photo for ID card |
| 10:00 AM | Laptop handover & IT setup (Okta, MFA, Slack, email) |
| 11:30 AM | Welcome session by People Ops |
| 1:00 PM | Lunch with your buddy |
| 2:30 PM | Meet your manager; 30-60-90 day plan |
| 4:00 PM | Mandatory training enrolment on LearnSphere |

Your **ID card** is ready within 3 working days. Until then, use a temporary visitor badge from reception.

## Mandatory trainings (complete within 30 days)
| Training | Duration | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Code of Conduct & ORBIT values | 45 min | LearnSphere |
| Information Security Awareness | 60 min | LearnSphere |
| POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) | 45 min | LearnSphere |
| Data Privacy (GDPR & DPDP Act) | 40 min | LearnSphere |
| Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption | 30 min | LearnSphere |
| Secure Coding (engineers only) | 90 min | LearnSphere |

Overdue mandatory training blocks your eligibility for the next performance cycle and is escalated to your manager after 45 days.

## Buddy programme
Every new joiner gets a buddy (from a different team) for the first 60 days. Your buddy's name is in your welcome email.

## Probation
- Probation is **6 months** for L1–L6 and **3 months** for L7+.
- Confirmation is automatic unless the manager raises a concern before the end of probation. Confirmation letter is available in PeopleHub.
- Notice period during probation: **15 days** (both sides).

## First 90 days checklist
- [ ] Week 1: set up your 1:1 cadence with your manager
- [ ] Week 1: join Slack channels #announcements, #ask-anything, #it-help, #hr-help, your team channel
- [ ] Week 2: complete mandatory trainings
- [ ] Week 2: claim home office setup (HR-WFH-001)
- [ ] Month 1: agree on OKRs for the quarter
- [ ] Month 2: 60-day check-in with HRBP
- [ ] Month 3: 90-day feedback conversation
