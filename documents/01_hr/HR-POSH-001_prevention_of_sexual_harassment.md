# POSH Policy

## 1. Scope
Applies to all Orbitra workplaces including offices, client sites, offsites, business travel, company events, virtual meetings, Slack and any work-related communication, regardless of time or place.

## 2. What constitutes sexual harassment
Unwelcome physical contact or advances; demand or request for sexual favours; sexually coloured remarks or jokes; showing pornography; any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature, including online.

## 4. Filing a complaint
- File in writing to icc@orbitra.io within **3 months** of the incident (extendable by another 3 months for valid reasons).
- If the complainant cannot file, a friend, relative or colleague may file with their consent.
- Interim relief (transfer, leave up to 3 months, restraint on the respondent) can be requested.

## 5. Inquiry process
1. IC acknowledges within **2 working days**.
2. Conciliation may be attempted only if the complainant requests it (no monetary settlement).
3. Inquiry is completed within **90 days**; report within 10 days after.
4. Action recommended within 60 days of the report.

## 6. Confidentiality and non-retaliation
Identities, statements and outcomes are strictly confidential. Retaliation against complainants or witnesses is itself misconduct.

## 7. False complaints
Malicious complaints made with knowledge of falsity may attract action. Inability to prove a complaint is **not** a false complaint.

## 8. Training
POSH training is mandatory within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter.
