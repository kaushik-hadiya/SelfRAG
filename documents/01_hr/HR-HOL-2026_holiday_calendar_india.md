# Holiday Calendar 2026 – India

India employees get **10 fixed holidays** (location-specific) and **2 floating holidays** chosen from the optional list. Floating holidays must be applied in PeopleHub → Leave → "Floating Holiday" at least 3 days in advance. Unused floating holidays lapse on 31 December.

Note: Where a fixed holiday falls on a weekend (Independence Day, Karnataka Rajyotsava, Diwali), no substitute holiday is given. Telangana Formation Day (02-Jun) is a holiday for Hyderabad in place of the second Diwali day.

## Optional / floating holiday list (choose any 2)
Eid-ul-Fitr (21-Mar), Good Friday (03-Apr), Eid-ul-Adha (27-May), Muharram (26-Jun), Raksha Bandhan (28-Aug), Onam (26-Aug), Janmashtami (04-Sep), Guru Nanak Jayanti (24-Nov), Your birthday, Your work anniversary.

## Year-end
The office remains open between Christmas and New Year, but a voluntary **"Quiet Week"** applies from 28–31 December: no internal meetings are scheduled and deployments are frozen (see ENG-004).
