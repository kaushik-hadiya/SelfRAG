# ESOPs, RSUs and Recognition

## 1. Equity plans
| Entity | Plan | Instrument |
|---|---|---|
| India | Orbitra ESOP Plan 2021 | Stock options |
| US / UK | Orbitra Global Equity Plan 2022 | RSUs |

### Vesting schedule (standard)
- **4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff**: 25% vests on the 1st anniversary of the grant date; the remaining 75% vests **quarterly** over the next 3 years (6.25% per quarter).
- Refresh grants (from annual cycle) vest over 4 years with no cliff.

### ESOP exercise (India)
- Vested options can be exercised during **exercise windows** held in **June and December** each year via the Equity portal (equity.orbitra.internal).
- Exercise price is as per your grant letter.
- Perquisite tax applies on exercise (difference between FMV and exercise price) and is deducted via payroll.
- After exit, vested options can be exercised within **5 years** of the leaving date (extended from 90 days in 2024). Unvested options lapse on the LWD.

### Buyback
The company conducted ESOP liquidity programmes in 2023 and 2025. Future programmes are announced in #announcements; no commitment is made on frequency.

## 2. Recognition programmes
| Programme | Value | Who nominates |
|---|---|---|
| Kudos (Slack #kudos) | Points redeemable on the Rewards store (1 point = INR 1) — 500 points per kudos from managers | Anyone / managers |
| Spot Award | INR 10,000 / USD 150 / GBP 120 | Manager (approved by L8) |
| ORBIT Values Award (quarterly) | INR 50,000 + trophy | Peers, chosen by department heads |
| Founders' Award (annual) | INR 2,00,000 + 2 extra days of leave | ELT |
| Long Service Award | 5 years: INR 25,000 voucher; 10 years: INR 1,00,000 + 1-week paid sabbatical | Automatic |

Rewards above INR 5,000 are taxable as perquisites in India and processed through payroll.
