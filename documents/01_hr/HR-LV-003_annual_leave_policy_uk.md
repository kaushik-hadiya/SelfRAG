# Annual Leave and Absence Policy – UK

## Annual leave
- **25 days** annual leave plus the **8 UK bank holidays**.
- One additional day of leave for each completed year of service, up to a maximum of 30 days.
- Leave year: 1 January – 31 December.
- Up to **5 days** may be carried over; carried-over days must be used by **31 March**.
- The office closes between Christmas and New Year; 3 days of annual leave are reserved for this closure.

## Sickness absence
- Notify your manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of absence.
- Self-certify for up to 7 calendar days; a fit note from a GP is required after that.
- Company sick pay: full pay for up to **20 working days** in any rolling 12 months, then Statutory Sick Pay (SSP).

## Family leave
- **Maternity / Adoption:** 26 weeks at full pay, followed by 13 weeks at SMP rate (total 39 weeks paid), up to 52 weeks in total.
- **Paternity / Partner leave:** 6 weeks at full pay.
- **Shared Parental Leave:** available per statutory rules; enhanced to full pay for the first 6 weeks.

## Other
- Compassionate leave: 5 days (immediate family).
- Dependants' leave: reasonable unpaid time off for emergencies.
