# Payroll, Salary Structure and Tax – India

## 1. Payroll calendar
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Payroll input cut-off (leave, LOP, reimbursements, new joiners) | **20th** of the month |
| Salary credit | **Last working day** of the month |
| Payslip available on PeopleHub | By the **3rd** of the following month |
| Form 16 issued | By **15 June** each year |

Joiners who join after the 20th are paid for their partial month along with the next month's salary.

## 2. Salary structure (illustrative components)
| Component | Rule |
|---|---|
| Basic | 50% of fixed CTC |
| HRA | 50% of Basic. Note: for the income-tax HRA exemption (old regime only), Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad are treated as non-metro, so the exemption limit is 40% of Basic. |
| Special allowance | Balancing figure |
| Employer PF | 12% of Basic, capped at Basic of INR 15,000 unless employee opts for full PF |
| Gratuity | 4.81% of Basic (payable after 5 years of continuous service) |
| Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP) | Fuel & vehicle, telephone/internet, meal card (Zeta), books & periodicals, LTA – declared at start of FY |
| Variable pay | 10% (L1–L5), 15% (L6–L7), 20% (L8+) of CTC; paid annually in June based on company and individual performance |

## 3. Tax regime and investment declaration
- New tax regime is the default. You may switch to the old regime in PeopleHub → Tax → Regime Selection **once per FY during the April window (1–30 April)**.
- **Investment declaration window:** 1–30 April (provisional).
- **Proof submission window:** **5 January – 31 January**. Proofs not submitted by 31 January lead to higher TDS deduction in February and March.
- Rent receipts: PAN of landlord is mandatory if annual rent exceeds INR 1,00,000.
- Previous-employer income (Form 12B) must be submitted within 30 days of joining.

## 4. Provident Fund (PF)
- Your UAN is shown on your payslip. Activate it on the EPFO member portal.
- PF transfer from a previous employer: initiate on EPFO portal; Orbitra approves within 7 working days. Raise OrbitDesk → Payroll → "PF transfer" if pending longer.
- Voluntary PF (VPF) can be changed twice a year (April and October).

## 5. Common requests
| Request | How |
|---|---|
| Change bank account | PeopleHub → My Profile → Bank Details. Upload a cancelled cheque. Changes made after the 20th apply from next month. |
| Salary certificate / employment letter | PeopleHub → Letters → Generate (instant, digitally signed) |
| Payslip for older months | PeopleHub → Payroll → Payslips (last 36 months) |
| Salary advance | Up to 1 month's net salary, interest-free, recovered over 6 months. Only for medical or family emergencies. Raise OrbitDesk → Payroll → "Salary advance"; needs HRBP approval. One active advance at a time. |
| Discrepancy in salary | OrbitDesk → Payroll → "Salary discrepancy" before the 10th of the next month; corrections are paid as an off-cycle run on the 15th. |

## 6. Gratuity
Payable at exit after 5 years of continuous service: (15 × last drawn Basic × completed years) / 26, subject to the statutory cap of INR 20,00,000.
