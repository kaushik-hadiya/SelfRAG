# Performance Management and Promotions

## 1. Review cycles
Orbitra runs **two review cycles per year**:
| Cycle | Review period | Self-review window | Ratings shared | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-year | Apr–Sep | **1–10 October** | by 15 November | Feedback + mid-year promotions (limited) |
| Annual | Oct–Mar | **1–10 April** | by 15 May | Rating, increment, promotion, variable pay |

Employees who joined less than **90 days** before the review period ends are not rated in that cycle.

## 2. Process
1. **Self-review** in PeopleHub → Performance (achievements against OKRs, ORBIT values).
2. **Peer feedback:** choose 3–5 peers; manager approves the list.
3. **Manager review** and proposed rating.
4. **Calibration** by department leadership to ensure consistency.
5. **1:1 conversation** to share rating and feedback.

## 3. Rating scale
| Rating | Label | Typical distribution guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | ~10% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | ~25% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | ~50% |
| 2 | Partially meets | ~12% |
| 1 | Does not meet | ~3% |

## 4. Increments
- Annual increments are effective **1 April** and paid from the **June** payroll with arrears for April and May.
- Increment guidance (FY27 India): rating 5 → 12–18%; 4 → 9–12%; 3 → 6–8%; 2 → 0–3%; 1 → 0%.
- Increment letters are available in PeopleHub → Letters after 15 May.

## 5. Promotions
- Eligibility: minimum **12 months** in current level and rating of **4 or above** in the latest annual cycle (rating 3 eligible only with VP exception).
- Promotion nominations are made by the manager with a promotion case document (template on Confluence: "Promo Case Template").
- Promotions carry a **minimum 10% increase** over current fixed pay in addition to the cycle increment.

## 6. Rating disagreement
If you disagree with your rating, raise it with your HRBP within **15 days** of the rating being shared. A skip-level review is conducted within 30 days. The decision after skip-level review is final.

## 7. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
- Initiated only after documented feedback over at least one quarter.
- Duration 60 days with weekly check-ins and clear, measurable goals.
- HRBP is present at the start and end of the PIP.

## 8. Continuous feedback
Use **Kudos** in Slack (#kudos) or PeopleHub → Feedback anytime. Managers must hold a 1:1 at least every 2 weeks.
