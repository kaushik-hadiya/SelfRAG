# PTO and Leave Policy – United States

## PTO
| Years of service | Annual PTO |
|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 20 days |
| 3–5 years | 23 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days |

- PTO accrues per pay period (semi-monthly, 24 periods).
- Up to **5 days** of unused PTO carry over to the next calendar year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.
- Unused PTO is paid out at termination where required by state law (Texas does not require it, but Orbitra pays out up to 10 days as company practice).

## Sick time
10 paid sick days per year, front-loaded on 1 January (pro-rated for new hires). Does not carry over. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Holidays
11 company holidays: New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day. Plus 1 floating holiday.

## Parental leave
- **16 weeks fully paid** for birthing parents (includes short-term disability benefit).
- **12 weeks fully paid** for non-birthing parents, including adoptive and foster parents.
- Must be taken within 12 months of the birth/placement; may be split into at most 2 blocks.

## Other leaves
- **FMLA:** up to 12 weeks job-protected unpaid leave for eligible employees (12 months service, 1,250 hours).
- **Bereavement:** 5 days (immediate family), 2 days (extended).
- **Jury duty:** fully paid for up to 10 days.
- **Voting:** up to 2 hours paid.

## How to request
Request PTO in PeopleHub at least 2 weeks in advance for 5+ consecutive days. Contact your HRBP Rachel Kim (rachel.kim@orbitra.io) for leaves of absence.

Note: US employees do not receive Earned Leave, Casual Leave or comp-off — these are India-only leave types.
