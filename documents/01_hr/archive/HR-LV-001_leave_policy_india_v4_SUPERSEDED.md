# Leave Policy – India (v4.0) — ARCHIVED / SUPERSEDED

> ⚠️ This version is retained for audit purposes only. It was superseded on 1 April 2026 by v5.0. Do not use it to answer current questions.

## Leave types and entitlement (as of 2024)
| Leave type | Entitlement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Earned Leave | 18 days | Carry forward max **25 days** |
| Sick Leave | 12 days | Lapses on 31 Dec |
| Casual Leave | 6 days | Lapses on 31 Dec |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks | — |
| Paternity Leave | **5 working days** | Use within 3 months of birth |
| Bereavement Leave | 3 working days | Immediate family only |
| Marriage Leave | 3 working days | — |
| Comp-off | 1 day | Use within **30 days** |

Planned leave of more than 3 days must be applied 7 days in advance.
