# Referral Programme and Internal Mobility

## Part A – Employee Referral Programme

### Referral bonus (India)
| Level of the hired candidate | Bonus |
|---|---|
| L1–L4 | INR 50,000 |
| L5–L7 | INR 1,00,000 |
| L8 and above | INR 1,50,000 |
| Hard-to-fill roles (tagged 🔥 on the jobs page) | +50% on the above |

US: USD 2,000 (L1–L5), USD 4,000 (L6+). UK: GBP 1,500 (L1–L5), GBP 3,000 (L6+).

### Rules
- Submit referrals in **PeopleHub → Careers → Refer**. A referral is valid for **6 months** from submission.
- If the candidate already applied or is in our database (last 6 months), the referral is not valid.
- The bonus is paid in the payroll after the referred employee completes **90 days** of employment, provided the referrer is still employed and not serving notice.
- Not eligible: recruiters, hiring managers for their own roles, L9 and above, and referrals of former employees who left within the last 12 months.
- Interns hired as full-time: no referral bonus.

## Part B – Internal Job Posting (IJP)

### Eligibility
- Minimum **12 months** in current role (18 months for L1 graduate hires).
- Latest annual performance rating of **3 or above**; not on a PIP.
- Applicable to roles at the same level or one level up.

### Process
1. Browse open roles at PeopleHub → Careers → Internal Jobs (internal roles are posted internally **10 days** before external posting).
2. **Inform your current manager** before applying (mandatory; the manager cannot block the application but may agree a release date).
3. Interview with the hiring team.
4. If selected, release within **60 days** maximum (30 days typical).
5. Compensation is reviewed by Total Rewards; a lateral move does not change pay unless a level change occurs.

### Relocation (internal transfer to another city)
- Relocation allowance: one month's fixed gross (capped at INR 1,50,000), economy flights for family, packers & movers up to INR 75,000, and 14 days of company-paid accommodation.
- International relocation is handled case-by-case by Global Mobility (mobility@orbitra.io).
