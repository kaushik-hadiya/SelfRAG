# Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Office attendance
| Location | Expectation |
|---|---|
| Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad | **3 days per week** in office. **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are anchor days.** |
| Austin | 2 days per week (Tuesday and Wednesday) |
| London | 2 days per week (team-chosen) |
| Customer Support night shift | Fully remote allowed |
| Fully remote employees (approved at hire) | Visit office once per quarter for team week (travel reimbursed) |

Attendance is tracked through badge swipes. Managers review team attendance monthly; consistent non-compliance (below 8 office days in a month for India) is discussed with the HRBP.

## 2. Work From Home (temporary)
Beyond the hybrid schedule, an employee may request temporary WFH for personal reasons (e.g., caring for a sick family member, home repairs) by marking **"WFH Request"** in PeopleHub → Attendance. Manager approval is required. Not counted against leave.

## 3. Work From Anywhere (WFA)
- Up to **20 working days per calendar year** from any location **within the employee's country of employment**.
- Working from **another country** requires approval from HRBP **and** Legal (tax/immigration), must be requested **30 days in advance**, and is capped at **14 days per year**. Not permitted to sanctioned countries.
- Must have a reliable connection and be available during core hours.

## 4. Home office support
| Item | India | US | UK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internet allowance | INR 1,500 / month (paid with salary) | USD 50 / month | GBP 30 / month |
| One-time home office setup | INR 15,000 (chair, desk, monitor) on joining | USD 500 | GBP 400 |
| Monitor | Request via IT (IT-002) – 24" monitor shipped home | same | same |

Claim one-time setup via SpendWise → "Home Office Setup" within 90 days of joining. Receipts required.

## 5. Security when working remotely
- Always connect via Orbitra SecureConnect VPN for internal systems (see IT-003).
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without VPN. Do not let family members use your company laptop.
- Restricted data must never be printed at home.
