# Resignation and Exit Process

## 1. Notice period
| Category | Notice period |
|---|---|
| During probation (all levels) | 15 days |
| L1–L5 (India, confirmed) | **60 days** |
| L6–L10 (India, confirmed) | **90 days** |
| US (at-will) | 2 weeks requested |
| UK | As per contract: 1 month (L1–L5), 3 months (L6+) |

## 2. How to resign
1. Discuss with your manager first (recommended).
2. Submit resignation in **PeopleHub → Separation → Resign**. Your LWD is calculated automatically.
3. Your manager and HRBP have 5 working days to hold a retention conversation (optional).

## 3. Early release and notice buyout
- Early release is at the manager's discretion, subject to knowledge transfer.
- If not released early, you may **buy out** the shortfall: amount = (Basic + HRA) / 30 × days short. Recovered in FnF.
- EL balance **cannot** be adjusted against notice period.

## 4. Clearance checklist
| Item | Owner | When |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop, charger, accessories, ID card | IT / Admin | On LWD (remote: courier pickup arranged 3 days before LWD) |
| Knowledge transfer document | Manager | Before LWD |
| Outstanding advances / corporate card settlement | Finance | Before LWD |
| Access revocation | IT (automatic at 6 PM on LWD) | LWD |
| Exit interview | HRBP | Last week |

## 5. Full and Final settlement (FnF)
- Processed within **45 days** of LWD.
- Includes: salary till LWD, EL encashment (max 30 days, see HR-LV-001), pro-rated variable pay (if eligible as per plan), gratuity (5+ years), minus recoveries (notice shortfall, advances, unreturned assets at book value, clawbacks for L&D/joining bonus).
- FnF statement emailed to personal email.

## 6. Documents
- **Relieving letter** and **experience letter**: issued on the LWD (digitally) if clearance is complete.
- **Form 16** for the partial year: issued by 15 June of the following FY.
- **PF**: transfer to new employer via EPFO; withdrawal allowed after 2 months of unemployment.

## 7. Rehire
Former employees who left in good standing with a rating ≥ 3 are eligible for rehire after 6 months.
