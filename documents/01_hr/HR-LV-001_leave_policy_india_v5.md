# Leave Policy – India (v5.0)

> **What changed in v5.0 (effective 1 April 2026):** Paternity leave increased from 5 to **10 working days**; new **Adoption leave** and **Pet bereavement leave (1 day)**; EL carry-forward cap increased from 25 to **30 days**. The previous version (v4.0) is archived and no longer applies.

For US employees see HR-LV-002. For UK employees see HR-LV-003.

## 1. Leave year
The leave year runs **1 January – 31 December**. (Note: this differs from the financial year, which runs April–March.)

## 2. Leave types and entitlement
| Leave type | Entitlement per year | Accrual | Carry forward | Encashable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earned Leave (EL) | 18 days | 1.5 days credited on the 1st of every month | Yes, max **30 days** total balance into next year | Yes, at exit only, max 30 days on Basic salary |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 12 days | Full 12 credited on 1 January (pro-rated for joiners) | No | No |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 6 days | Full 6 credited on 1 January (pro-rated) | No | No |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks (first two children); 12 weeks thereafter | — | — | — |
| Paternity Leave | **10 working days** | Per child | Must be used within 6 months of birth | No |
| Adoption Leave | 12 weeks for the primary caregiver (child of any age up to 5 years); 10 working days for the secondary caregiver | Per adoption | Must start within 3 months of placement | No |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 working days (immediate family); 2 days (extended family) | Per event | — | No |
| Pet Bereavement | 1 day | Per event | — | No |
| Marriage Leave | 3 working days | Once during employment | Must be used within 30 days of wedding | No |
| Miscarriage Leave | 6 weeks | Per event | — | No |
| Voting Leave | Half day on polling day | — | — | — |
| Comp-off | 1 day per weekend/holiday worked (min 4 hours) | Approved by manager | Must be used within **60 days** | No |
| Sabbatical | Up to 3 months unpaid | After 5 continuous years of service | — | — |

"Immediate family" means spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law. "Extended family" means grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, cousin.

## 3. Applying for leave
1. Apply in **PeopleHub → Leave → Apply Leave**. Your manager is the approver.
2. Planned leave of **more than 3 consecutive working days** must be applied at least **7 calendar days in advance**.
3. Sick leave of more than **2 consecutive days** requires a medical certificate uploaded in PeopleHub within 3 days of returning.
4. Leave not approved or rejected by the manager within 3 working days is **auto-approved**.
5. Weekends and public holidays falling between leave days are **not** counted as leave (sandwich rule does not apply at Orbitra).
6. Half-day leave is allowed for EL, SL and CL.

## 4. Probation
Employees on probation (first 6 months) accrue EL normally but may use a maximum of **6 days of EL** during probation. SL and CL are available pro-rated.

## 5. Negative balance and LOP
- EL balance cannot go below zero. Leave taken without balance is treated as **Loss of Pay (LOP)**.
- LOP is deducted in the payroll of the same month if marked before the payroll cut-off (20th), else in the next month.
- Unapproved absence for 3+ consecutive days without communication may be treated as unauthorised absence (see HR-COC-001).

## 6. Maternity leave details
- Eligible after 80 days of service in the 12 months preceding the expected delivery date.
- Up to 8 of the 26 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date.
- After maternity leave, the employee may opt for **work-from-home for up to 3 months** with manager agreement, and a **phased return (4-day week at full pay) for 4 weeks**.
- Crèche reimbursement: up to INR 8,000 per month per child until the child turns 5 (claim via SpendWise, category "Crèche").

## 7. Leave encashment at exit
At exit, up to 30 days of EL balance is encashed at Basic salary / 30 per day as part of the Full and Final settlement (see HR-EXIT-001). SL, CL and comp-off are not encashed.

## 8. Holidays
India offices follow the published holiday calendar (HR-HOL-2026): 10 fixed holidays + 2 floating (optional) holidays.

## 9. FAQs
**Can I take EL in advance?** No. Only credited EL can be used.
**What if I fall sick during EL?** You can request your manager to convert EL days to SL with a medical certificate.
**Do I lose CL at year end?** Yes, CL and SL lapse on 31 December.
**Where do I check my balance?** PeopleHub → Leave → My Balances. If it looks wrong, raise OrbitDesk → HR → "Leave balance correction".
