# Code of Conduct

Every Orbitra employee, intern and contractor must read and acknowledge this Code annually in LearnSphere (acknowledgement window: **1–31 January**).

## 1. Respectful workplace
Orbitra has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, bullying or retaliation based on gender, caste, religion, race, age, disability, sexual orientation, marital status, or any protected attribute. Sexual harassment is governed by HR-POSH-001.

## 2. Conflict of interest
You must disclose in PeopleHub → Declarations → Conflict of Interest:
- A close relative working at Orbitra, a customer, a vendor, or a competitor.
- Any financial interest above 1% in a vendor, customer or competitor.
- Board positions in any for-profit company.
Managers may not directly supervise a relative or romantic partner.

## 3. Outside employment (moonlighting)
- Paid work for another organisation, including freelancing, is **not permitted** without written approval from your HRBP and department head.
- Unpaid open-source contributions, teaching, writing and non-profit work are allowed **outside working hours**, provided they do not use Orbitra confidential information or compete with Orbitra. Contributions to open-source projects that relate to Orbitra's products require approval from the Open Source Program Office (ospo@orbitra.io).

## 4. Gifts and hospitality
- Gifts **to or from** customers, vendors or partners must not exceed **INR 5,000 / USD 75 / GBP 60** per person per year.
- Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers) are never allowed.
- Anything above the limit must be declared in the **Gift Register** (PeopleHub → Declarations) within 7 days and may need to be returned.
- No gifts to government officials without Legal approval (see LEG-002 Anti-Bribery).

## 5. Social media and public statements
- Only Corporate Communications and authorised spokespersons speak on behalf of Orbitra.
- You may say you work at Orbitra, but add "views are my own".
- Never share customer names, roadmap, financials, or internal screenshots publicly.
- Media enquiries → press@orbitra.io.

## 6. Use of company assets
Company assets are for business use. Limited personal use of laptops is acceptable provided it complies with IT-005 Acceptable Use Policy.

## 7. Dress code
Smart casual. Business formal for customer meetings and external events. Fridays are casual.

## 8. Attendance and absence
Unauthorised absence for **3 or more consecutive working days** without informing your manager may lead to disciplinary action. Absence for 7+ days without communication may be treated as abandonment of employment.

## 9. Alcohol and substances
No alcohol on premises except at company-organised events with approval. Working under the influence of drugs or alcohol is prohibited.

## 10. Reporting violations
Report concerns to your manager, HRBP, ethics@orbitra.io, or anonymously through the **Ethics Hotline** (see LEG-003 Whistleblower Policy). Retaliation against anyone reporting in good faith is a serious violation.

## 11. Disciplinary action
Violations can lead to verbal warning, written warning, suspension, or termination depending on severity, decided by the **Disciplinary Committee** (HR, Legal, department head).
