# Employee Benefits and Insurance – India (Policy year Apr 2026 – Mar 2027)

## 1. Group Medical Insurance (GMC)
- **Insurer:** SecureLife General Insurance Co. | **TPA:** CareBridge TPA
- **Sum insured:** **INR 5,00,000 family floater**
- **Covered:** Employee + spouse + up to 2 children (or same-sex/live-in partner with declaration)
- **Premium:** fully paid by Orbitra
- Day-1 coverage; pre-existing diseases covered from day 1; no waiting period.
- Maternity: covered up to INR 75,000 (normal) / INR 1,00,000 (C-section) within the floater.
- Room rent: up to 2% of sum insured per day (normal room), 4% for ICU.
- Pre- and post-hospitalisation: 30 and 60 days.
- OPD: not covered under GMC (see Wellness wallet below).

### Parental insurance (optional, employee-paid)
- Separate floater of **INR 3,00,000** for parents **or** parents-in-law (not both).
- Annual premium **INR 14,500**, deducted from salary in 12 monthly instalments.
- Enrolment only within 30 days of joining or during the annual window (**1–15 March**).
- 10% co-pay applies.

### Super top-up (optional)
INR 10,00,000 over and above the floater, deductible INR 5,00,000. Premium INR 3,200/year.

## 2. How to use health insurance
**Cashless (planned):** Get pre-authorisation from CareBridge TPA at least 48 hours before admission using your e-card (download from the CareBridge app or PeopleHub → Benefits).
**Cashless (emergency):** Hospital desk raises pre-auth with your e-card within 24 hours of admission.
**Reimbursement:** Submit claim on the CareBridge app within **30 days of discharge** with original bills, discharge summary, and prescriptions.
**Network hospitals:** Search on the CareBridge app. 9,000+ hospitals.
**Helpline:** CareBridge 24x7: 1800-200-4455 | Orbitra benefits desk: benefits@orbitra.io

**Adding a newborn or spouse mid-year:** Add within **30 days** of the event (birth/marriage) via PeopleHub → Benefits → Life Event. Otherwise you must wait for the March window.

## 3. Group Term Life Insurance
3x annual fixed CTC (minimum INR 30 lakh). Nominee update in PeopleHub → Benefits.

## 4. Group Personal Accident
5x annual fixed CTC; covers accidental death and permanent disability, 24x7 worldwide.
