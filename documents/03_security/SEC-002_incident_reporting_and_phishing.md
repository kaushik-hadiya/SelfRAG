# Reporting Security Incidents and Phishing

## 1. What to report
- Suspicious emails, SMS, WhatsApp or calls (phishing, vishing, "CEO asking for gift cards").
- Clicking a suspicious link or entering your password on a suspicious page.
- Malware alerts, unexpected pop-ups, unknown software installed.
- Lost or stolen laptop, phone, or YubiKey (also see IT-002).
- Data sent to the wrong recipient, or customer data exposed.
- Unauthorised person in the office.

## 2. How to report — within **1 hour** of noticing
| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Suspicious email (not clicked) | Click **"Report Phish"** button in Gmail. That's it. |
| Clicked a link / entered credentials | Immediately **reset your Okta password**, then call the Security hotline **Ext. 4911** (+91-80-4719-4911, 24x7) |
| Any other incident | Email **security@orbitra.io** **and** post in Slack **#sec-incidents** (do not share sensitive details in the channel) |
| Data breach involving customer personal data | Call Ext. 4911 immediately. Do not inform the customer yourself — Legal and Security handle notifications (GDPR requires regulator notification within 72 hours). |

You will **never** be penalised for reporting in good faith, even if you made the mistake.

## 3. How to spot phishing
- Sender domain looks similar but wrong (orbitra-hr.com, 0rbitra.io). Only **@orbitra.io** is ours.
- Urgency or secrecy: "do this now, don't tell anyone".
- Requests for gift cards, wire transfers, bank detail changes, or OTPs.
- Unexpected attachments (.html, .zip, .iso) or QR codes.
- Executives will **never** ask you to buy gift cards or share OTPs over WhatsApp.

## 4. Vendor bank detail change requests
Any email asking to change a vendor's bank account must be verified by **calling the vendor on a known number** (not the number in the email). Finance must follow FIN-003 §6.

## 5. What happens after you report
Security triages within 30 minutes (24x7). You may be asked to hand over your laptop for forensic analysis; a loaner will be provided.
