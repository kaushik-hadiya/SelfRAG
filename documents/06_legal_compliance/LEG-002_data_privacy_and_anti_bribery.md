# Data Privacy and Anti-Bribery

## Part A – Data Privacy
Orbitra complies with the **EU/UK GDPR**, India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**, and US state privacy laws (e.g., CCPA). Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) is **Ishita Sen** (dpo@orbitra.io).

### Key principles
- Collect only the personal data needed for a defined purpose.
- Do not move personal data to new tools without a privacy review (OrbitDesk → Security → "Vendor review").
- Customer data is processed only per the customer's instructions (we are a **processor** for customer data and a **controller** for employee data).

### Data Subject Requests (DSARs)
If a customer, candidate or anyone asks to access, correct or delete their personal data, **forward the request to privacy@orbitra.io within 24 hours**. Do not respond yourself. Statutory deadlines: GDPR – 1 month; DPDP – as prescribed.

### Data retention (selected)
| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Employee records | 8 years after exit |
| Candidate data (not hired) | 12 months, unless consent to retain |
| Customer data after contract end | Deleted within 90 days |
| Slack messages | 2 years (channels), 1 year (DMs) |
| CCTV footage | 30 days |

### Employee privacy
Your personal data is processed per the **Employee Privacy Notice** (Confluence → Legal). You can request a copy of your data via privacy@orbitra.io.

## Part B – Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption (ABAC)
Orbitra complies with the Prevention of Corruption Act (India), the US FCPA and the UK Bribery Act 2010.
- Never offer, give, request or accept anything of value to improperly influence a business decision.
- **Facilitation payments** ("speed money") are prohibited, even if small or customary.
- **Government officials** (including employees of state-owned enterprises): no gifts, meals or hospitality without prior written approval from Legal.
- Third-party agents and resellers must undergo ABAC due diligence before onboarding.
- Gifts and hospitality limits are in HR-COC-001 §4.
- Report concerns to ethics@orbitra.io or the Ethics Hotline (LEG-003).
