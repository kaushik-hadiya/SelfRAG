# Contracts, NDAs and Signing Authority

## 1. NDAs
- **Use Orbitra's standard NDA template** (mutual or one-way) available on Confluence → Legal → Templates. If the counterparty accepts our template without changes, **no Legal review is needed** — send via **DocuSign** using the "Standard NDA" workflow and it is auto-signed by the Legal Ops signatory.
- If the counterparty sends their own NDA or edits ours, raise OrbitDesk → Legal → "NDA review". Turnaround: **2 business days**.
- NDAs must be in place before sharing Confidential information (see SEC-001).

## 2. Contract review
All contracts (customer MSAs, order forms with non-standard terms, vendor agreements, partnerships, data processing agreements) must be reviewed by Legal before signing.
| Contract type | Turnaround |
|---|---|
| Standard order form on Orbitra paper | Self-serve via Salesforce CPQ (no review) |
| Customer MSA redlines | 5–7 business days |
| Vendor agreements | 7–10 business days |
| DPA / security addendum | 5 business days (with Security) |

## 3. Signing authority
Only authorised signatories can sign on behalf of Orbitra. **Employees must never sign contracts personally**, including click-through terms for paid tools.
| Contract value (annual) | Authorised signatory |
|---|---|
| NDA (standard) | Legal Ops (auto via DocuSign) |
| Up to USD 50,000 | VP of the function + Legal |
| USD 50,001 – 500,000 | CFO or COO |
| Above USD 500,000 | CEO |

## 4. Contract repository
All signed contracts are stored in **Ironclad** (contracts.orbitra.internal). Search by counterparty. Access is limited to Legal, Finance and the contract owner.
