# Whistleblower (Speak Up) Policy

## What can be reported
Fraud, financial misreporting, bribery, theft, data misuse, serious safety risks, violations of law or the Code of Conduct, and retaliation. (Personal grievances such as compensation disagreements should go to your HRBP; sexual harassment to the IC per HR-POSH-001.)

## How to report
| Channel | Details |
|---|---|
| Ethics Hotline (independent, anonymous) | **speakup.orbitra-ethics.com** or phone 000-800-919-1234 (India toll-free), 1-844-555-0199 (US), 0800-0-555-019 (UK). Operated by an independent third party, 24x7, multiple languages. |
| Email | ethics@orbitra.io (read by General Counsel and Head of Internal Audit) |
| Audit Committee Chair (for complaints against ELT) | audit-committee@orbitra.io |

You may remain anonymous. Anonymous reporters receive a case number and PIN to follow up through the hotline.

## Investigation
- Acknowledgement within **5 working days**.
- Investigations are led by Internal Audit and/or Legal, typically completed in **45 days**.
- The reporter is informed when the case is closed (details of disciplinary action are confidential).

## Protection
Orbitra prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith. Retaliation should itself be reported and will result in disciplinary action up to termination.
