# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Principles
Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be business-related, reasonable, approved and supported by valid receipts.

## 2. How to claim
1. Submit claims in **SpendWise** (spendwise.orbitra.internal or the mobile app — snap a photo of the receipt).
2. **Submit within 30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than **60 days** are rejected automatically unless approved by the Financial Controller.
3. Receipts are mandatory for any expense **above INR 500**. Below INR 500, a self-declaration is accepted (max INR 3,000 per month).
4. Approval flow: Manager → Finance audit. Amounts above the manager's authority go to the next level (see COM-002 approval matrix).
5. **Payment:** Claims fully approved by the **20th** are paid with that month's salary. Claims approved after the 20th are paid in the next month's salary. US/UK: paid by bank transfer in the next weekly run (every Friday).

## 4. Not reimbursable
Personal travel, traffic or parking fines, alcohol without a client present, gym memberships (use wellness wallet), laundry (except travel > 4 nights), in-room movies, minibar, personal grooming, gifts to colleagues, tips above 10%.

## 5. Common reasons for rejection
- Missing or unreadable receipt; receipt not in your name for flights/hotels.
- Category mismatch (e.g., internet claimed in SpendWise).
- Duplicate submission.
- Over limit without approval.
If rejected, you get an email with the reason; **edit and resubmit within 7 days**.

## 6. Advances
Travel advances can be requested in SpendWise → "Advance" for international travel only (max 70% of estimated per diem). Advances must be settled within **15 days** of return; unsettled advances are recovered from salary.
