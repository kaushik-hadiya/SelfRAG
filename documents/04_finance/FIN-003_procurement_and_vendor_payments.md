# Procurement and Vendor Payments

## 1. When do you need a Purchase Request (PR)?
Any purchase of goods or services from a vendor, **including SaaS subscriptions**, must go through a PR in **NetSuite Procurement** (via OrbitDesk → Finance → "Purchase Request"), except:
- Expenses covered by FIN-001 (personal reimbursements), and
- Corporate card purchases under INR 25,000 / USD 300 (see FIN-004).

**No PO, no pay:** invoices without a valid Purchase Order (PO) are not paid.

## 2. Quotation requirements
| Purchase value (annual) | Quotes needed |
|---|---|
| Up to INR 2,00,000 | 1 quote |
| INR 2,00,001 – 15,00,000 | 2 quotes |
| Above INR 15,00,000 | 3 quotes + Procurement-led negotiation |

## 3. Approval flow
Requester → Budget owner (per COM-002 matrix) → Procurement → Security review (if the vendor will access Internal or higher data, see SEC-001) → Legal (contract) → Finance (PO issued). Typical turnaround: **7–15 business days**.

## 4. Vendor onboarding
Ask Procurement to send the vendor the onboarding link. Vendor must submit: PAN, GST certificate, cancelled cheque, MSME certificate (if applicable), W-9/W-8 (US). Vendors cannot be paid until onboarding is complete.

## 5. Invoices and payment terms
- Vendors email invoices to **invoices@orbitra.io** with the PO number.
- Standard payment terms: **Net 45** days from invoice receipt. MSME vendors (India): **Net 45 maximum by law**, paid within 30 days as Orbitra practice.
- Payment runs: every **Tuesday and Friday**.
- To check payment status, ask the vendor to email ap-queries@orbitra.io with the invoice number, or check NetSuite → Vendor Bills.

## 6. Vendor bank detail changes
Bank changes require: a request on vendor letterhead, a cancelled cheque, and **call-back verification** by AP on the number in the vendor master (never the number in the email). This is mandatory due to frequent fraud attempts (see SEC-002).

## 7. Software purchases
Before buying a new SaaS tool, check whether an existing approved tool covers the need (IT-005). All software purchases need IT approval for SSO integration and licence management.
