# Corporate Card Policy

## Eligibility
- Employees at **L7 and above**, and
- Employees at any level who travel internationally more than 4 times a year or manage department subscriptions (on L8 recommendation).

Apply via OrbitDesk → Finance → "Corporate card request". Card issued by **HDFC Bank (India)** / **American Express (US/UK)** in 10 business days.

## Limits
| Level | Monthly limit |
|---|---|
| L1–L6 (exception holders) | INR 1,00,000 / USD 2,000 |
| L7–L8 | INR 3,00,000 / USD 5,000 |
| L9+ | INR 10,00,000 / USD 15,000 |

Single transaction above INR 25,000 / USD 300 for goods or software requires a PO (FIN-003).

## Rules
- Business use only; no cash withdrawals; no personal expenses even if repaid.
- Reconcile every transaction in SpendWise with a receipt **within 15 days** of the transaction.
- Card is **suspended** if transactions remain unreconciled for 45 days.
- Lost card: call the bank immediately (HDFC 1800-202-6161 / Amex number on the back), then inform finance-cards@orbitra.io.
- Card must be surrendered at exit; outstanding unreconciled amounts are recovered in FnF.
