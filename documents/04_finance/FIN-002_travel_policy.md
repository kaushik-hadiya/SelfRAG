# Business Travel Policy

## 1. Booking
- All flights and hotels must be booked through **Orbitra Travel** (travel.orbitra.internal), managed by our travel partner **JourneyPro**. Self-booked travel is reimbursed only if it is cheaper than the Orbitra Travel quote and pre-approved.
- Book domestic travel at least **7 days** and international travel at least **21 days** in advance.
- JourneyPro 24x7 helpline: +91-80-6900-1122 | orbitra@journeypro.travel
- Approvals: domestic travel — manager; **international travel — L8 (Director) approval** in addition to manager.

## 2. Class of travel
| Mode | L1–L7 | L8 | L9–L10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic flight | Economy | Economy (premium economy if > 3 hours) | Business |
| International flight < 6 hours | Economy | Economy | Business |
| International flight ≥ 6 hours | Economy | Premium economy | Business |
| Train (India) | 2AC / Vande Bharat chair car | 1AC | 1AC |

## 3. Hotel limits (per night, excluding taxes)
| City tier | L1–L5 | L6–L7 | L8+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 India (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai) | INR 6,000 | INR 8,000 | INR 12,000 |
| Tier 2 India (all other cities) | INR 4,000 | INR 5,500 | INR 8,000 |
| US (NYC, SF) | USD 300 | USD 350 | USD 450 |
| US (other cities) | USD 200 | USD 250 | USD 325 |
| UK (London) | GBP 200 | GBP 240 | GBP 300 |
| Europe (other) | EUR 180 | EUR 220 | EUR 280 |
| Singapore / Dubai | USD 250 | USD 300 | USD 380 |

Use company-preferred hotels shown on Orbitra Travel where available (they include breakfast). Staying with friends/family: no hotel cost, but you may claim **INR 1,500 per night** "home stay allowance" (domestic only).

## 4. Per diem (meals and incidentals)
| Travel | L1–L5 | L6–L7 | L8+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic (India) | INR 1,500/day | INR 2,000/day | INR 2,500/day |
| International | USD 60/day | USD 75/day | USD 90/day |

- Per diem is paid **without receipts**; claim in SpendWise → "Per diem" with travel dates.
- Day of departure and return: 50% if travel starts after 2 PM or ends before 12 PM.
- If meals are provided (conference, client), deduct 30% per provided meal.

## 5. Ground transport
Airport transfers by cab (actuals). Car rentals (US/UK) allowed for L6+ or when cheaper than cabs; compact/mid-size only. Personal car usage: INR 12/km (India), USD 0.67/mile (US IRS rate).

## 6. Travel insurance and safety
- Travel medical insurance for international trips is automatic when booked through Orbitra Travel. Download the certificate from the booking.
- Register international trips on the **SafeTravel** portal for emergency assistance.
- Travel to high-risk countries needs CISO + CFO approval.

## 7. Visa
JourneyPro handles business visa applications. Raise the request at least 30 days before travel; invitation letters are issued by HR (OrbitDesk → HR → "Visa letter").

## 8. Changes and cancellations
Cancellation fees are borne by the company when due to business reasons; for personal reasons, the employee bears them. Personal extension of a business trip ("bleisure") is allowed up to 3 days with manager approval; extra hotel nights and any fare difference are paid by the employee.
