# Learning & Development Policy

## 1. Annual learning budget
| Country | Budget per employee per financial year (Apr–Mar) |
|---|---|
| India | **INR 50,000** |
| US | USD 1,500 |
| UK | GBP 1,200 |

- Available after **confirmation** (i.e., after probation).
- Covers: online courses, books, certifications, workshops, conference tickets (conference travel is charged to the team's travel budget per FIN-002).
- Unused budget **does not carry over**.
- Pro-rated for employees confirmed mid-year.

## 2. Free learning platforms (no budget used)
All employees have free access via **LearnSphere** to: Udemy Business, Coursera for Business (500 seats — request via #learning), O'Reilly Learning (engineering), LinkedIn Learning.

## 3. Certifications
- **Pre-approval** from the manager is required in LearnSphere → "Certification request" before booking the exam.
- The exam fee is **reimbursed only after passing**, claimed via SpendWise → "Learning & Development" with the certificate and invoice, within 60 days of passing.
- **One retake** is reimbursed if the first attempt fails, provided the retake is passed.
- Priority certifications (reimbursed **outside** the annual budget): AWS Solutions Architect (Associate/Professional), AWS Security Specialty, CKA/CKAD, Google Professional Cloud Architect, CISSP, PMP, Salesforce Administrator (Sales Ops).

## 4. Higher education sponsorship
- Up to **INR 3,00,000 per year** (max 2 years) for part-time degree programmes relevant to the role (e.g., executive MBA, M.Tech).
- Eligibility: 2+ years at Orbitra, rating ≥ 4 in the last cycle, approval by L9 and CHRO.

## 5. Clawback
If you resign within **12 months** of reimbursement for a certification or course above INR 25,000 (or within **24 months** for higher education sponsorship), the amount is recovered pro-rata from the Full and Final settlement (see HR-EXIT-001).

## 6. Learning time
Employees can use up to **5 working days per year** as "Learning Days" for structured learning (mark in PeopleHub → Attendance → "Learning Day"), with manager approval.

## 7. Internal programmes
- **LeadStart** (first-time managers, L6–L7) — 8-week cohort, nomination by HRBP, twice a year.
- **Tech Talks** — every Friday 4 PM IST in #tech-talks.
- **Mentorship programme** — apply in April and October on LearnSphere.
